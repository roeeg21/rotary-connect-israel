**Working Name:** Rotary Connect Israel

**Alternative names:** Rotary Network, Rotary Connections, Rotary Link Israel

**Platform:** iOS, Android, Responsive Web

**Languages:** Hebrew + English

**Primary Organization:** Rotary Israel — District 2490

**Document Version:** 1.0



---



# 1. Product Vision



**Rotary Connect Israel** is a private professional and personal connection network for the Rotary community in Israel.



The purpose is simple:



> **Find the right person, through Rotary.**



A member who needs access to a company, profession, area of expertise, organization, industry, geographic location, or specific role should be able to search the Rotary network and quickly identify the people who can help.



For example:



* “Who works at Microsoft?”

* “Who knows someone at Elbit?”

* “Find a cardiologist in Haifa.”

* “Who works in real estate in Tel Aviv?”

* “I need someone involved in venture capital.”

* “Who is a lawyer specializing in commercial law?”

* “Who works in the Ministry of Education?”

* “Find a member who works in cybersecurity.”

* “Who is a CEO or senior manager in the food industry?”

* “Does anyone in Rotary have a connection to Samsung Korea?”



The application transforms Rotary Israel from a list of members into a **living network of relationships, expertise and opportunities**.



It should strengthen one of Rotary’s most valuable assets:



**the people inside the organization.**



---



# 2. Core Product Principle



The application is **not intended to become another Facebook or LinkedIn**.



There should be:



* no endless social feed;

* no follower counts;

* no likes;

* no popularity rankings;

* no unnecessary posting;

* no advertising;

* no clutter.



The application revolves around three actions:



**SEARCH → DISCOVER → CONNECT**



Everything else is secondary.



---



# 3. Target Community



The network consists of:



### Primary members



Verified members of Rotary clubs in Israel.



### Family network



Rotary members may invite:



* spouse / partner;

* adult children;

* adult immediate family members where permitted by Rotary Israel policy.



Family members must always be visibly identified as connected to a Rotary member.



Example:



**Dana Cohen**

Marketing Director · Strauss Group

Family of David Cohen

Rotary Club Haifa



For privacy and safeguarding, users under 18 should not appear as searchable professional contacts or receive unsolicited contact requests. If Rotary wishes to associate minors with a family account, their information should remain private and non-searchable.

# 4. Product Goals



## Primary goals



1. Increase the practical value of Rotary membership.

2. Make the professional power of the Rotary community visible.

3. Help members reach people and organizations through trusted connections.

4. Encourage members to help other members.

5. strengthen relationships across clubs.

6. Create meaningful connections between members who otherwise would never meet.

7. Give younger members an additional reason to join Rotary.

8. Connect different generations of Rotary families.

9. Build a continuously improving professional knowledge graph of Rotary Israel.

10. Strengthen the feeling:



**“Being part of Rotary gives me access to a trusted network.”**

# 34. Anti-Spam Rules



The network should never become a sales database.



Rules should prohibit:



* mass messaging;

* scraping;

* harvesting contact information;

* automated sales outreach;

* exporting the complete directory;

* marketing lists;

* unsolicited bulk promotions.



Rate limits should apply to new contact requests.



Repeated abuse can trigger:



Warning → Restriction → Suspension.



---

A member only knows another user was interested when that person sends a contact or introduction request.

# 58. Product Summary



**Rotary Connect Israel is a private, verified, bilingual relationship and expertise network for Rotary Israel and its families.**



It converts information that is currently fragmented across clubs, personal relationships, WhatsApp groups and individual knowledge into a searchable network.



The app should be extraordinarily easy to understand:



### Search someone.



### Discover who can help.



### Make the connection.



And visually it should represent a **new generation of Rotary**:



professional but not corporate,

modern but not trendy,

minimal but not empty,

distinctly Rotary without looking old-fashioned.



**Rotary Connect should feel less like a social network and more like having the entire Rotary Israel community in your contacts.**

You are acting as a senior product designer, UX/UI designer, frontend architect, and design-system specialist.



Your task is to completely redesign the existing app I provide.



Before making changes, inspect the entire project carefully:



* Existing pages

* Components

* Navigation

* User flows

* Current styles

* Responsive behavior

* Existing assets and logo

* Current framework and libraries

* Any design system already in use



You are allowed to install, download, or use any skills, MCP tools, UI libraries, icon libraries, components, packages, or supporting tools you need in order to produce the best result.



If useful, install appropriate design/UI skills before beginning.



Do not simply improve the existing colors or spacing.



I want a meaningful redesign of the whole user experience.



## Main Design Direction



The app should feel like a premium modern social network.



Think about the usability and polish of products such as:



* LinkedIn

* Instagram

* Airbnb

* Notion

* Linear

* modern Apple applications



Use these only as references for quality, interaction patterns, clarity, and polish.



DO NOT copy them.



The final product must have its own identity.



The design must NOT look like a typical AI-generated application.



Avoid:
